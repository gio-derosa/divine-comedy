La Divina Commedia

Italiano: the text of Giorgio Petrocchi's critical edition, La Commedia secondo l'antica vulgata (Le Lettere, Firenze, 1994), as transcribed on Italian Wikisource.

English: Henry Wadsworth Longfellow's translation (1867), from Project Gutenberg eBook #1004.

The two texts are independent: the Italian tab shows Dante's text and the English tab shows Longfellow's. The line-by-line translations revealed by tapping a line are literal study aids written for this site, not part of either published text.
