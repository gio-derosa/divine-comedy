1|'Through me one goes into the sorrowful city,
2|through me one goes into the eternal sorrow,
3|through me one goes among the lost people.
4|Justice moved my high maker;
5|the divine power made me,
6|the highest wisdom and the first love.
7|Before me there were no things created
8|if not eternal, and I eternal last.
9|Abandon every hope, you who enter'.
10|These words of dark color
11|I saw written at the top of a gate;
12|wherefore I: "Master, their meaning is hard for me".
13|And he to me, like a perceptive person:
14|"Here it is fitting to abandon every suspicion;
15|every cowardice it is fitting that here be dead.
16|We have come to the place where I have told you
17|that you will see the sorrowful people
18|who have lost the good of the intellect".
19|And after he had put his hand on mine
20|with a glad face, whence I was comforted,
21|he put me inside to the secret things.
22|There sighs, laments and loud wails
23|resounded through the air without stars,
24|so that I at the beginning wept at it.
25|Diverse tongues, horrible speeches,
26|words of pain, accents of anger,
27|voices loud and faint, and sound of hands with them
28|were making a tumult, which whirls
29|always in that air forever dyed,
30|like the sand when the whirlwind breathes.
31|And I, who had my head girt with error,
32|said: "Master, what is that which I hear?
33|and what people is it that seems so vanquished by pain?".
34|And he to me: "This miserable mode
35|the wretched souls of those maintain
36|who lived without infamy and without praise.
37|Mingled they are with that base choir
38|of the angels who were not rebels
39|nor were faithful to God, but were for themselves.
40|The heavens chase them out so as not to be less beautiful,
41|nor does the deep hell receive them,
42|for the guilty would have some glory from them".
43|And I: "Master, what is so heavy
44|to them that it makes them lament so strongly?".
45|He answered: "I will tell it to you very briefly.
46|These have no hope of death,
47|and their blind life is so low,
48|that envious they are of every other lot.
49|Fame of them the world does not allow to be;
50|mercy and justice disdain them:
51|let us not speak of them, but look and pass".
52|And I, who looked again, saw a banner
53|that, circling, was running so swift,
54|that of any rest it seemed to me unworthy;
55|and behind it there came so long a train
56|of people, that I would not have believed
57|that death had undone so many.
58|After I had recognized some there,
59|I saw and knew the shade of him
60|who made through cowardice the great refusal.
61|Immediately I understood and was certain
62|that this was the sect of the base ones,
63|displeasing to God and to his enemies.
64|These wretches, who were never alive,
65|were naked and stung much
66|by gadflies and by wasps that were there.
67|These streaked their face with blood,
68|which, mingled with tears, at their feet
69|by loathsome worms was gathered up.
70|And after I gave myself to looking beyond,
71|I saw people at the bank of a great river;
72|wherefore I said: "Master, now grant me
73|that I may know who they are, and what custom
74|makes them seem so ready to cross over,
75|as I discern through the faint light".
76|And he to me: "The things will be made known to you
77|when we will stop our steps
78|on the sad shore of Acheron".
79|Then with eyes ashamed and low,
80|fearing lest my speaking be burdensome to him,
81|until the river I withdrew from speaking.
82|And behold toward us coming by boat
83|an old man, white with ancient hair,
84|crying: "Woe to you, wicked souls!
85|Do not hope ever to see the heaven:
86|I come to lead you to the other bank
87|into the eternal darkness, into heat and into frost.
88|And you who are there, living soul,
89|depart from these who are dead".
90|But after he saw that I did not depart,
91|he said: "By another way, by other ports
92|you will come to shore, not here, for passing:
93|a lighter vessel must carry you".
94|And the leader to him: "Charon, do not torment yourself:
95|it is willed so there where one can do
96|what one wills, and ask no more".
97|Hence were quieted the woolly cheeks
98|of the pilot of the livid marsh,
99|who around the eyes had wheels of flames.
100|But those souls, who were weary and naked,
101|changed color and gnashed their teeth,
102|as soon as they heard the cruel words.
103|They blasphemed God and their parents,
104|the human species and the place and the time and the seed
105|of their sowing and of their births.
106|Then they drew back all of them together,
107|loudly weeping, to the evil shore
108|which awaits every man who does not fear God.
109|Charon demon, with eyes of burning coal
110|beckoning them, gathers them all;
111|he beats with the oar whoever lingers.
112|As in autumn the leaves lift away
113|one after the other, until the branch
114|sees on the ground all its spoils,
115|similarly the evil seed of Adam
116|throw themselves from that shore one by one,
117|at signals like a bird at its call.
118|So they go away over the dark wave,
119|and before they have landed over there,
120|already on this side a new throng gathers.
121|"My son", said the courteous master,
122|"those who die in the wrath of God
123|all gather here from every land;
124|and ready they are to cross the river,
125|for the divine justice spurs them,
126|so that the fear turns into desire.
127|From here never passes a good soul;
128|and therefore, if Charon complains of you,
129|well can you know now what his speech means".
130|This finished, the dark countryside
131|trembled so strongly, that of the terror
132|the memory with sweat still bathes me.
133|The tearful land gave forth wind,
134|which flashed a vermilion light
135|which overcame in me every sense;
136|and I fell like the man whom sleep seizes.
