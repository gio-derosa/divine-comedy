1|Absorbed in his delight, that contemplative
2|freely assumed the office of teacher,
3|and began these holy words:
4|"The wound that Mary closed and anointed,
5|she who is so beautiful at her feet
6|is the one who opened it and who pierced it.
7|In the order that the third seats make,
8|sits Rachel below her
9|with Beatrice, as you see.
10|Sarah and Rebecca, Judith and she
11|who was great-grandmother to the singer who for sorrow
12|for his fault said 'Have mercy on me',
13|you can see thus from tier to tier
14|descending downward, as I with their proper names
15|go down through the rose from petal to petal.
16|And from the seventh step downward, just as
17|down to it, follow Hebrew women,
18|dividing all the tresses of the flower;
19|because, according to the look that made
20|the faith in Christ, these are the wall
21|by which the sacred stairs are divided.
22|On this side where the flower is mature
23|with all its petals, are seated
24|those who believed in Christ to come;
25|on the other side where are interrupted
26|with empty spaces the semicircles, are
27|those who had their faces turned to Christ already come.
28|And as on this side the glorious seat
29|of the lady of heaven and the other seats
30|below it make so great a division,
31|so opposite does that of the great John,
32|who always holy the desert and martyrdom
33|suffered, and then hell for two years;
34|and below him thus were allotted to divide
35|Francis, Benedict and Augustine
36|and others down to here from circle to circle.
37|Now behold the high divine providence:
38|for the one and the other aspect of the faith
39|will equally fill this garden.
40|And know that from the tier downward which strikes
41|midway along the two divisions,
42|they are seated for no merit of their own,
43|but for that of others, with certain conditions:
44|for all these are spirits released
45|before they had true choice.
46|Well can you perceive it by their faces
47|and also by their childish voices,
48|if you look at them well and if you listen to them.
49|Now you doubt and doubting are silent;
50|but I will loosen the strong bond
51|in which your subtle thoughts bind you.
52|Within the amplitude of this realm
53|a point of chance cannot have place,
54|any more than sadness or thirst or hunger:
55|for by eternal law is established
56|whatever you see, so that exactly
57|here the ring corresponds to the finger;
58|and therefore this people hastened
59|to true life is not without cause
60|among themselves here more and less excellent.
61|The king through whom this kingdom rests
62|in so great love and in so great delight,
63|that no will dares for more,
64|all the minds in his glad aspect
65|creating, at his pleasure with grace endows
66|diversely; and here let the effect suffice.
67|And this expressly and clearly is noted for you
68|in the holy Scripture in those twins
69|who in their mother had their anger stirred.
70|Therefore, according to the color of the hair,
71|with such grace the most high light
72|must worthily crown them.
73|Therefore, without merit of their conduct,
74|they are placed on different tiers,
75|differing only in their first sharpness of sight.
76|It sufficed in the early centuries
77|with innocence, to have salvation,
78|only the faith of the parents;
79|after the first ages were completed,
80|it was needful for males for their innocent wings
81|through circumcision to acquire power;
82|but after the time of grace came,
83|without perfect baptism of Christ
84|such innocence was held back down there.
85|Look now at the face that to Christ
86|most resembles, for its brightness
87|alone can dispose you to see Christ".
88|I saw upon her such great gladness
89|raining, borne in the holy minds
90|created to fly across that height,
91|that whatever I had seen before,
92|did not hold me suspended in such wonder,
93|nor showed me so much semblance of God;
94|and that love which first descended there,
95|singing 'Hail, Mary, full of grace',
96|before her spread his wings.
97|Answered the divine chant
98|from all sides the blessed court,
99|so that every face became more serene from it.
100|"O holy father, who for me endure
101|to be down here, leaving the sweet place
102|in which you sit by eternal lot,
103|who is that angel who with such joy
104|looks into the eyes of our queen,
105|so enamored that he seems of fire?".
106|Thus I had recourse again to the teaching
107|of him who grew beautiful from Mary,
108|as from the sun the morning star.
109|And he to me: "Boldness and grace
110|as much as can be in angel and in soul,
111|all is in him; and so we want it to be,
112|because he is the one who carried the palm
113|down to Mary, when the Son of God
114|willed to load himself with our burden.
115|But come now with your eyes as I
116|go on speaking, and note the great patricians
117|of this most just and pious empire.
118|Those two who sit up there most happy
119|for being nearest to the Empress,
120|are of this rose as it were two roots:
121|he who is set beside her on the left
122|is the father through whose daring taste
123|the human species tastes so much bitterness;
124|on the right you see that ancient father
125|of Holy Church to whom Christ the keys
126|entrusted of this lovely flower.
127|And he who saw all the grievous times,
128|before he died, of the beautiful bride
129|who was won with the lance and with the nails,
130|sits beside him, and beside the other rests
131|that leader under whom lived on manna
132|the people ungrateful, fickle and backsliding.
133|Opposite Peter you see Anna sitting,
134|so content to gaze at her daughter,
135|that she does not move her eye to sing hosanna;
136|and opposite the greatest father of a family
137|sits Lucy, who moved your lady
138|when you were bending, to your ruin, your brows.
139|But because the time flies that makes you sleep,
140|here we will make a stop, like a good tailor
141|who according as he has cloth makes the gown;
142|and we will direct our eyes to the first love,
143|so that, looking toward him, you may penetrate
144|as far as is possible into his brightness.
145|Truly, lest perhaps you go backward
146|moving your wings, believing you go beyond,
147|by praying grace must be obtained,
148|grace from her who can help you;
149|and you will follow me with your affection,
150|so that from my speaking you do not part your heart".
